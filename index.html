I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Tongji University. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Minnesota with Prof. Douglas N. Arnold, as part of the Simons Collaboration on Localization of Waves. I obtained my Ph.D. degree in mathematics from the Pennsylvania State University in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. John Harlim. Here is my CV (updated May 2026) and Google Scholar profile.

My broad research interests lie in numerical analysis and applied mathematics, with a recent focus on developing numerical methods for solving partial differential equations (PDEs) on point clouds and on investigating localization phenomena in disordered media.

Research Interests

  • Scientific computing
  • Wave localization
